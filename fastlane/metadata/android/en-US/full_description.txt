Marculus is a forest tree-marking app for phones and tablets. It replaces the notebook and the tally counter with a marking sheet: a species × diameter-class matrix where every tree is counted and stays traceable.

Who it is for: marking foresters, forest experts, private managers, municipalities and firewood-lot holders (French "affouage"). The app is in French and English.

<b>In the field</b>
• Species × class sheet, one tap per tree, undo kept in the history
• Height and quality for each tree
• Offline voice dictation: "beech forty five windfall" (French vocabulary)
• Append-only tree log: every tree has its own unique ID

<b>Volumes</b>
• Schaeffer volume tables (fast, intermediate, slow, very slow) and EMERGE (226 species)
• Stem, crown and total volumes, basal area
• Firewood lots: capped volume per lot, lot summary, stacked cubic metres

<b>Map and GNSS</b>
• GeoPackage import: parcels, forest roads, orthophoto
• GNSS position for each tree, external RTK/NTRIP receiver supported
• Each tree is automatically assigned to its parcel

<b>Sharing without a server</b>
• CSV export
• .marsync files to merge the work of several markers
• Import of a batch of worksites (.zip)

<b>Network: what goes out, what does not</b>
Marculus works offline, with no ads, no account and no tracking. Two features need the network, only when you start them: online map tiles (IGN Plan, IGN orthophoto, OpenStreetMap) and the download of the French voice model (about 42 MB) from Settings.
